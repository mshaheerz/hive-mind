**Twitter Thread:**

1/5 Introducing the Team Retro Dashboard - a lightweight, self-hosted solution for quick & anonymous team retrospectives. No accounts, no fuss. Create a board, share the link, and start collecting feedback in under 60 seconds! #teamretro #agile #productivity

2/5 Ready to get started? Clone and run the codebase in 5 minutes: `git clone`, `docker compose up -d`, and http://localhost:3000 will be up and running! #devops #docker

3/5 With the Team Retro Dashboard, you can:

Create boards with templates
Share links for anonymous feedback
Vote and group topics
Export to CSV or PDF for meeting notes

4/5 The API is open for integrations and bots! Base URL: `http://localhost:3000/api` | Method | Endpoint | Description | POST | `/boards` | Create board `{name, template}` |

5/5 Join the community! Contribute to the project, report issues, or suggest features. We welcome PRs! Small issues are labeled “good first issue”. Let's make team retrospectives easier and more efficient! #open-source #contributing

Are you tired of lengthy team retrospectives that derail your workflow? Look no further! The Team Retro Dashboard is a self-hosted, lightweight solution that makes it easy to collect anonymous feedback from your team in under 60 seconds.
